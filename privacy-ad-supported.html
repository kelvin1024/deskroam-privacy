Privacy Policy

DeskRoam connects devices you control directly. The developer does not operate a service that receives your screen, audio, clipboard, files, or input.

Last updated October 20, 2026. This policy applies to the free, ad-supported version of DeskRoam for iPhone and iPad and its companion Mac Host. It applies when you use that version, including a test build. The policy for the version without advertising is available separately.

DeskRoam does not require an account. Remote-session content travels directly between your devices over an encrypted connection. Google advertising and consent services process information separately as described below. Every remote feature is free; an optional one-time purchase removes ads.

Who this policy covers

This policy covers the free, ad-supported DeskRoam app for iPhone and iPad and the companion DeskRoam Host app for Mac. “Developer” means the publisher of DeskRoam, reachable at j1024kh@gmail.com. The Mac Host does not contain an advertising SDK.

Your remote-session content

The developer does not provide DeskRoam accounts or operate a backend that receives your device-to-device session content. Depending on the features you enable, your direct connection can carry:

  • the Mac’s screen and optional Mac audio to your iPhone or iPad;
  • keyboard, pointer, scroll, shortcut, and system-command input to your Mac;
  • clipboard text when you choose to send it or enable clipboard synchronization; and
  • files you explicitly select to send to the Mac.

This content provides the feature you requested. It is sent directly between your devices using TLS with a pre-shared key derived from the pairing code or trusted-device credential. A device without the shared secret cannot complete the encrypted connection. DeskRoam does not route this content through a developer server. Screen frames, audio packets, and remote-input events are used during the active connection and are not recorded by DeskRoam.

Data stored on your devices

  • Keychain: pairing secrets and trusted-device credentials.
  • App preferences: settings, keyboard layouts, saved Mac names and addresses, selected options, and recent connection details.
  • Received files: completed files are saved in the DeskRoam folder inside your Mac’s Downloads folder and revealed in Finder. Incomplete transfers are removed.
  • Clipboard: text is placed on the destination device’s system clipboard. DeskRoam does not keep a separate clipboard history.

Free features and optional ad removal

Mac connection and every remote feature are free. Advertising availability, privacy choices, a canceled purchase, or an unavailable ad service do not prevent remote use. An optional one-time, non-consumable purchase removes advertising. It is not a subscription and does not unlock additional remote features. The App Store displays the applicable price.

Apple processes purchases and payment information through StoreKit. DeskRoam checks Apple-signed transaction entitlements on the device and does not send receipts to a developer server. Restore Purchases checks ad-removal ownership for the current Apple Account. DeskRoam does not receive your payment-card details.

Advertising and consent information

The iPhone and iPad app integrates Google Mobile Ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). A banner can appear below the remote-control area when advertising is available and your privacy choices permit it. Google and its advertising services may process the following information to provide and measure advertising, maintain security and performance, and provide privacy choices:

  • Approximate location: information inferred from your IP address or network, including to determine applicable privacy choices.
  • Device and app identifiers: identifiers used for ad delivery, reporting, security, and fraud prevention.
  • Advertising data: ad impressions, delivery, and interactions.
  • Product interactions: interactions with advertising and consent messages, including privacy choices.
  • Crash data: information about failures affecting advertising or consent services.
  • Performance data: information about how those services operate.
  • Other diagnostic data: information used to identify and resolve issues with those services.

See Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services for its processing and controls. The consent message provides applicable advertising-partner information and choices.

DeskRoam does not supply Mac screen images, audio, keystrokes, clipboard text, transferred-file contents, saved Mac details, or pairing credentials as advertising targeting data. It does not supply advertising keywords, content URLs, custom user identifiers, or location targeting. No Firebase Analytics or mediation integration is configured.

DeskRoam requests non-personalized advertising and disables Google’s publisher first-party identifier. Non-personalized ads can still involve identifiers and information used for ad delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention. These settings do not eliminate Google’s data processing or replace applicable privacy choices.

Your advertising privacy choices

Before ad requests, the app obtains the UMP consent status and presents a required consent form where applicable. When Google’s consent service requires a privacy-options entry point, Advertising privacy options is available in Settings. It remains available after an ad-removal purchase if those choices were previously required. Consent or ad-loading errors leave remote features usable.

Google controls its own retention and deletion practices under its policies. Local DeskRoam unpairing, deleting app settings, or uninstalling DeskRoam does not delete information held by Google. An ad-removal purchase prevents further advertising in the app; it does not delete information already processed by advertising or consent services.

Mac permissions

DeskRoam Host asks macOS for Screen Recording permission to share the Mac display and Accessibility permission to apply remote keyboard and pointer input and, in app mode, resize the window you chose. The window’s original size and position are kept only in memory. Host attempts to restore them when app mode ends or the Host exits normally. Restoration depends on the app and available permissions and is not guaranteed after a crash or forced termination. You control these permissions in macOS System Settings and can revoke them at any time. Clipboard, audio, and file-transfer features are under your control in DeskRoam.

Retention and local deletion

There is no developer-held DeskRoam account or remote-session server record to delete. This does not apply to information you send to support or information processed by Apple or Google under their policies. Local settings remain until you change or remove them. You can:

  • delete a saved Mac or forget its pairing in DeskRoam on iPhone or iPad;
  • unregister one or all trusted devices in DeskRoam Host;
  • delete received files in Finder; and
  • revoke Screen Recording or Accessibility permission in macOS System Settings.

Uninstalling does not necessarily remove local preferences, pairing credentials, or received files. Apple operating systems may preserve Keychain items after an app is uninstalled. Use in-app unpairing controls before uninstalling when available, and delete the DeskRoam folder in Downloads to remove received files. For assistance with local data removal, email the developer.

Support communications

If you contact support, the developer receives the information you choose to include in that message and uses it to respond to and resolve your request. Do not send pairing codes, trusted-device credentials, clipboard contents, private files, or children’s personal information. Support communications are separate from the app’s direct remote connection.

Children

DeskRoam is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children.
