Privacy Policy

DeskRoam is designed to work directly between devices you control. The developer does not operate a service that receives your screen, audio, clipboard, files, or input.

Effective and last updated: October 5, 2026

In short: no developer data collection, no tracking, no advertising, no analytics, and no DeskRoam account. App settings and credentials stay on your devices. When you use a remote feature, its content travels directly between your iPhone or iPad and your Mac over an encrypted connection.

Who this policy covers

This policy covers the DeskRoam app for iPhone and iPad and the companion DeskRoam Host app for Mac. “Developer” in this policy means the publisher of DeskRoam.

Data the developer collects

The developer does not collect personal data through DeskRoam. DeskRoam has no developer-operated backend, user accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party telemetry SDK. The developer does not receive your device-to-device session content.

Data used between your devices

DeskRoam connects an iPhone or iPad to a Mac you choose. Depending on the features you enable, the connection can carry:

  • the Mac’s screen and optional Mac audio to your iPhone or iPad;
  • keyboard, pointer, scroll, shortcut, and system-command input to your Mac;
  • clipboard text when you choose to send it or enable clipboard synchronization; and
  • files that you explicitly select to send to the Mac.

This content is used only to provide the feature you requested. It is sent directly between your devices using TLS with a pre-shared key derived from the pairing code or trusted-device credential. A device without the shared secret cannot complete the encrypted connection. DeskRoam does not route this content through a developer server.

Data stored on your devices

DeskRoam stores only what it needs locally:

  • Keychain: pairing secrets and trusted-device credentials. StoreKit transaction entitlements are checked on the device; DeskRoam does not keep a local trial clock or trial record.
  • App preferences: settings, keyboard layouts, saved Mac names and addresses, selected options, and recent connection details are stored in the app’s local preferences.
  • Received files: a file you send is saved in the DeskRoam received-files folder inside the Mac app’s container and revealed in Finder. Incomplete transfers are removed.
  • Clipboard: clipboard text is placed on the destination device’s system clipboard. DeskRoam does not keep a separate clipboard history.

Screen frames, audio packets, and remote input events are used during the active connection and are not recorded by DeskRoam.

Purchases and subscription trial

Apple processes purchases through StoreKit and handles payment information, eligibility, billing, and subscription renewal. DeskRoam checks Apple-signed transaction entitlements on the device and does not send receipts to a developer server.

DeskRoam offers a monthly auto-renewable subscription with a seven-day introductory free offer for eligible Apple Accounts, followed by the monthly price shown by the App Store until canceled. Eligibility is determined by Apple; accounts that are not eligible can still choose the monthly subscription without a free trial. A separate non-consumable purchase permanently unlocks Mac connection and every feature. An active subscription or the permanent purchase grants access. When subscription access ends, Mac connections are blocked unless another qualifying purchase is owned.

Use of the apps is subject to the Apple standard End User License Agreement.

Permissions

DeskRoam Host asks macOS for Screen Recording permission to share the Mac display and Accessibility permission to apply remote keyboard and pointer input. You control these permissions in macOS System Settings and can revoke them at any time. Clipboard, audio, and file-transfer features are under your control in DeskRoam.

Retention and deletion

Because the developer does not receive DeskRoam data, there is no developer-held account or server record to delete. Local settings remain until you change or remove them. You can:

  • delete a saved Mac or forget its pairing in DeskRoam on iPhone or iPad;
  • unregister one or all trusted devices in DeskRoam Host on the Mac;
  • delete received files in Finder;
  • revoke Screen Recording or Accessibility permission in macOS System Settings; and
  • uninstall the apps to remove their app-container data.

Apple operating systems may preserve Keychain items after an app is uninstalled, including pairing credentials. Use the in-app unpairing controls before uninstalling when available. For assistance with local data removal, use the contact method on the Support page.

Sharing, sale, and tracking

The developer does not sell or share personal data, and does not track you across apps or websites. DeskRoam contains no ads.

Support communications

If you contact support, the developer receives the information you choose to include in that message. This information is not collected by the DeskRoam apps, is used only to respond to and resolve your request, and should not include pairing codes, trusted-device credentials, clipboard contents, or private files.

Children

DeskRoam is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children. Because the developer does not collect data through the app, DeskRoam does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If DeskRoam’s data practices change, this page will be updated before the change is released and the date above will be revised.
