Mac remote control

Your Mac, within reach.

Use your iPhone or iPad as a private screen, keyboard, trackpad, shortcut controller, clipboard, and file bridge for your Mac.

Direct by design: DeskRoam connects your devices directly over an encrypted connection. There is no DeskRoam account, advertising, analytics, or developer-operated relay server.

One app, two devices

Install DeskRoam on iPhone or iPad and the free DeskRoam Host on your Mac when available. DeskRoam Host is currently awaiting App Review, so its public download may not yet be available. Pair them with the code shown by the Host, then control your Mac from the touch-first remote interface.

Built for everyday control

  • View your Mac display and optional system audio.
  • Use touch as a trackpad with keyboard and shortcut controls.
  • Send clipboard text and selected files directly between devices.
  • Reconnect securely with credentials stored in Apple Keychain.

Choose monthly or permanent access

Choose a monthly auto-renewable subscription or a separate one-time purchase to connect to your Mac and use every feature. Eligible Apple Accounts may start the monthly subscription with a seven-day introductory free offer; Apple determines eligibility. After the offer, the subscription renews at the price shown by the App Store until canceled. You can also purchase permanent access once. When subscription access ends, connection requires an active subscription or a qualifying permanent purchase. Apple processes all payments and manages subscription eligibility and renewal.

In the U.S., the monthly plan is US$0.99 after any eligible seven-day free trial; permanent access is US$9.99 once. Prices vary by country or region. Read the Terms of Use (Apple standard EULA) and Privacy Policy.
