Mac remote control

Your Mac, within reach.

Use your iPhone or iPad as a private screen, keyboard, trackpad, shortcut controller, clipboard, and file bridge for your Mac.

This page describes the free, ad-supported version of DeskRoam for iPhone and iPad.

Direct by design: DeskRoam connects your devices directly over an encrypted connection, without a DeskRoam account or developer-operated relay. Remote features are free. Google advertising and consent services are separate from your remote-session content; see our privacy information.

One app, two devices

Install DeskRoam on iPhone or iPad and the free DeskRoam Host on your Mac, a notarized direct download. Pair them with the code shown by the Host, then control your Mac from the touch-first remote interface.

Built for everyday control

  • New — app mode: show one Mac app fitted to your iPhone or iPad, with zoom and text size remembered per app.
  • View your Mac display and optional system audio.
  • Use touch as a trackpad with keyboard and shortcut controls.
  • Send clipboard text and selected files directly between devices.
  • Reconnect securely with credentials stored in Apple Keychain.

Every remote feature free

Connect to your Mac and use every feature without a subscription or purchase. No banners are shown or requested for 24 hours after your first app launch. After that, a banner may appear on the Mac list before connection and below the connected remote-control area, separated from controls, when privacy choices and space permit. All features remain free after the first 24 hours. Missing ads or a privacy-consent error never prevents remote use.

Prefer no ads after the first 24 hours? An optional one-time Remove Ads purchase removes advertising, without adding or restricting remote features. It is available from the Mac list and Settings from your first launch; the purchase screen never opens automatically. There is no recurring charge. The App Store displays the applicable price, and ownership can be restored for the current Apple Account. Read the Terms of Use (Apple standard EULA) and Privacy Policy.
